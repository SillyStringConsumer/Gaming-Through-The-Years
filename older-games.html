Older Games

Older games can remain memorable because of their mechanics, atmosphere, music, and design—not simply nostalgia.

Why revisit classics?

Distinctive design

Older games often focus on a smaller set of mechanics that remain enjoyable years later.

A different kind of challenge

Older releases can be less forgiving, encouraging players to practice and experiment.

Preservation

Re-releases, backward compatibility, and PC storefronts can help keep older games accessible.

Classic games to revisit

Lost Planet: Extreme Condition combines science-fiction combat with enormous alien creatures and an icy setting. Silent Hill 2 is remembered for psychological horror and its unsettling atmosphere. Castlevania games showcase memorable action-platforming design, while Castle Crashers makes cooperative beat-em-up gameplay approachable and chaotic.

These games show how art direction and gameplay ideas can outlast the technology used to create them.
